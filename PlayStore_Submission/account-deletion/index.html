Fitora Account Deletion

Fitora respects your privacy and gives every user the ability to delete their account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Fitora app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

If you cannot access your account, you may request deletion by emailing:

Email: nagasubhash55@gmail.com

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User account
  • Email address
  • Fitness goals
  • Step history
  • Water tracking
  • Sleep records
  • Workout history
  • Profile information

Retention

Most user data is permanently deleted within 30 days after the deletion request unless retention is required by law.
