## Graphic Assets Required

> These must be uploaded to Play Console. Refer to `playstore assets/` folder in the project root.

| Asset | Size | Status |
|-------|------|--------|
| Feature Graphic | 1024 × 500 px | `playstore assets/Graphic.png` |
| Phone Screenshots (min 2, max 8) | 16:9 or 9:16 | `playstore assets/1.png` – `7.png` |
| Icon (hi-res) | 512 × 512 px | Generated from `assets/ICON.png` |
