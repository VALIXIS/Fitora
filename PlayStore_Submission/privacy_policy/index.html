1. Information We Collect

a) Account Information
When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your display name — used to personalise your greeting in the app
  • Your email address — used for authentication only
  • A unique Firebase UID — used to identify your session

b) Fitness & Activity Data
Fitora reads the following from your device's hardware sensors and from Android Health Connect (when permission is granted):

  • Daily step count
  • Distance walked or run
  • Active calories burned
  • Active minutes
  • Exercise sessions
  • Sleep duration and sessions
On-device processing: Fitness and activity data is processed directly on your device and displayed within the app. It is not transmitted to external servers.

c) Hydration Data
Water intake you manually log is stored locally on your device using Android's internal storage. This data is not transmitted externally.

d) Personal Preferences
Your age, height, weight, fitness goals, and notification preferences entered during onboarding are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your account and provide access to the app
  • Display personalised fitness metrics and progress summaries
  • Send hydration reminder notifications at your configured schedule
  • Improve your experience based on your stated fitness goals
We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Health Data & Health Connect

Fitora reads health and fitness data from Android Health Connect solely to display it within the app.

Data Type Read Write Purpose
Steps ✅ ❌ Merged with on-device sensor for step tracking
Distance ✅ ❌ Displayed in Progress analytics
Calories burned ✅ ❌ Shown on Home dashboard and Progress charts
Exercise sessions ✅ ❌ Populates workout history and weekly summaries
Sleep sessions ✅ ❌ Displayed in Health / Sleep section
Fitora does not write any data back to Health Connect. Health Connect data is never transmitted to external servers by Fitora. You may revoke Health Connect permissions at any time in your device's Health Connect settings.

4. Permissions Used

Fitora requests only the permissions necessary for its core features. Each permission is described below.

Permission Why It Is Required
ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION Required on Android 10+ to access the step counter and step detector hardware sensors for real-time step tracking.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to display hydration reminder notifications. Users can disable notifications in Settings at any time.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Android cancels all scheduled notifications on device reboot. This allows Fitora to reschedule hydration reminders automatically after restart.
VIBRATE Allows notification alerts to vibrate the device for users who keep their phone on silent mode.
health.READ_STEPS Read daily step data from Health Connect (optional — only when user connects Health Connect).
health.READ_DISTANCE Read distance from Health Connect for Progress analytics.
health.READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED Read active calories from Health Connect for the Home dashboard.
health.READ_EXERCISE Read exercise session records from Health Connect for workout history.
health.READ_SLEEP Read sleep session data from Health Connect for the Sleep section.

Fitora does not request: Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, External Storage, or Bluetooth permissions.

5. Firebase & Google Services

Fitora uses Firebase Authentication (provided by Google LLC) to manage user accounts. When you sign in with Google, your email address and a unique user ID are stored in Firebase Authentication.

Firebase is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Fitora does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. No usage statistics or crash reports are collected beyond what is strictly required for account authentication.

6. Data Security

  • Fitness, hydration, and preference data is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences and internal database storage.
  • Account data (email, user ID) is stored securely in Firebase Authentication, hosted on Google's infrastructure.
  • All communication between the app and Firebase uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • We do not operate our own servers or databases beyond Firebase Authentication.
  • Your Health Connect data is never transmitted outside your device by Fitora.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

The only third-party service that receives any of your data is:

  • Google / Firebase Authentication — receives your email address and user ID for account sign-in only. No other data is shared.

8. Children's Privacy

Fitora is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address in Section 11, and we will delete that information promptly.

9. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account data at any time by:

  1. Emailing us at [FILL IN SUPPORT EMAIL] with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Including the email address associated with your Fitora account

We will delete your Firebase Authentication record within 30 days of receiving your request.

Local data stored on your device (steps, hydration, preferences) can be deleted immediately by uninstalling the app.
